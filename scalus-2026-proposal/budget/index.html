Budget & oversight

Is my ADA safe?

The short answer is yes — and the reason is structural, not promissory. Treasury funds are held in an audited escrow contract with milestone-conditional disbursement, supervised by an independent oversight board of Cardano contributors who are not on the Lantr team. Below: oversight first, then the numbers.

Escrow mechanics

How disbursement actually works

Funds are held by the SundaeSwap treasury-contracts framework — the same framework being adopted across the 2025/2026 Cardano Treasury cycle, audited by TxPipe and MLabs. Disbursement is milestone-conditional and multi-signature.

Milestone-conditional disbursement

Each milestone is reviewed by the Independent Technical Assurer against pre-agreed acceptance criteria before the next disbursement is released. The contract enforces this at the script level — not as a process commitment.

Pause authority

Any individual oversight board member can pause a milestone disbursement unilaterally if delivery, integrity, or financial concerns arise. Resuming requires board majority. Routine disbursement requires Lantr + board majority co-signature.

Failsafe sweep

Funds remaining in the contract after the delivery window sweep back to the Cardano Treasury automatically. This is enforced at the contract level and cannot be overridden by Lantr or by the oversight board.

ADA price hedging

Funds are managed conservatively, with partial conversion into USD-denominated stablecoins where appropriate to protect runway against ADA drawdowns. The 10% refundable contingency provides additional buffer. Unspent contingency returns to Treasury.

Why this matters The 2025 cycle hit ADA price exposure on USD-referenced workplans. The 2026 design responds to that: a conservative $0.25/ADA reference rate as the floor on which budget was scoped, partial stablecoin hedging during execution, and a refundable contingency that returns to Treasury if unused.

Each quarter, a delivery report is published in the proposal repository covering engineering delivery, partner integrations, budget utilisation, and forward plan.

All inflows and disbursements logged using the SundaeSwap metadata standard. Every ADA movement linked back to a milestone or budget line, on-chain.

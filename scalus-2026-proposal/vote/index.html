For less-experienced DReps

How to vote — in three steps

1

Open Tempo

Open tempo.vote and connect the wallet associated with your DRep registration. Confirm you are on mainnet.

2

Find the action

Use the Vote on Tempo button above, or paste the Governance Action ID into Tempo search. The action title will read “Scalus: Cardano's Application Platform”.

3

Cast your vote

Choose Yes, No, or Abstain. Optionally attach a rationale CIP-100 metadata link. Confirm in your wallet. The vote is recorded on-chain within the next block.

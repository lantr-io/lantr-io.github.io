As part of our commitment to transparency in Cardano's decentralized governance, we're sharing progress on our Intersect-funded project: **Scalus - DApps Development Platform** (EC-0020-25).

<ZoomableImage src="/lantr-scalus-roadmap-122025.jpg" alt="Scalus Roadmap - December 2025" />

Milestone 2 focused on **adoption and onboarding** - making Scalus accessible to more developers and providing the resources needed to build production-ready smart contracts.

## What We Delivered

### Documentation & Onboarding

We reorganised [Scalus documentation](https://scalus.org/docs) providing:
- Tailored learning paths for [Cardano developers](https://scalus.org/docs/get-started/for-cardano-developers) starting with Scalus and [Scala developers](https://scalus.org/docs/get-started/for-scala-developers) entering Cardano
- [Language Guide](https://scalus.org/docs/language-guide) now includes detailed examples for every concept
- [Smart Contracts Guide](https://scalus.org/docs/smart-contract) walks through the full development workflow - from writing to testing and integration.

<ZoomableImage src="/scalus-documentation.png" alt="Scalus Documentation" />

### Rosetta Blueprints

We delivered 7 of 21 Rosetta smart contract blueprints - practical implementations of common use cases like **Betting**, **HTLC**, **Vesting**, **Vault**, and **Payment Splitter**. 
These serve as starting point references for developers building similar functionality.

The reference implementations are available in the [Scalus examples repository](https://github.com/scalus3/scalus/tree/master/scalus-examples/jvm/src/main/scala/scalus/examples). 

Note: In the upcoming milestones, we will contribute the finalised blueprints to the "Rosetta Blueprints" and "Cardano Blueprint and Ecosystem Monitoring" projects.

### Design Patterns & Security

We implemented 1st part of the design patterns from Anastasia Labs' catalogue, including **Stake Validator (Withdraw Zero Trick)**, **UTxO Indexer**, **Validity Range Normalization**, and **Transaction Level Minter** patterns. Each comes with documentation, examples, and tests. 

Check out the [Design Patterns](https://github.com/scalus3/scalus/tree/master/scalus-design-patterns) repository for more details.

We also added a [Plutus Vulnerabilities](https://scalus.org/docs/security/common-vulnerabilities) guide to help developers avoid common security pitfalls.

### Developer Templates

New command-line templates let developers scaffold projects instantly:
- `sbt new scalus3/hello.g8`  Hello World template
- `sbt new scalus3/validator.g8` - Starter validator template

Read more in our:
- [Getting Started Guide](https://scalus.org/docs/get-started)
- [Build your first Smart Contract](https://scalus.org/docs/smart-contracts/developing-smart-contracts)

### Ongoing Platform Improvements

Based on community feedback, we made numerous improvements to the Scalus platform: refined the smart contract syntax, enhanced the standard library with new math utilities and optimised operations, and improved the test kit for better developer experience.

**Scalus public releases**:
- 0.13.0 (2025-10-22)
- 0.12.1 (2025-10-08) 
- 0.12.0 (2025-09-27)

Complete changelog: [GitHub](https://github.com/scalus3/scalus/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md#0130-2025-10-22)

## Why This Matters

Cardano needs robust developer tooling to grow. Scalus enables full-stack dApp development in a single language—smart contracts, transaction building, backend, and frontend—all in Scala 3. The resources delivered in this milestone lower the barrier to entry and give developers production-ready patterns to build on.

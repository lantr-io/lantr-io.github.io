As part of our commitment to transparency in Cardano's decentralized governance, we're sharing progress on our Intersect-funded project: **Scalus – DApps Development Platform** (EC-0020-25).

<ZoomableImage src="/lantr-scalus-roadmap-122025.jpg" alt="Scalus Roadmap" />

Milestone 2 focused on adoption and onboarding—making Scalus accessible to more developers and providing the resources needed to build production-ready smart contracts.

## What We Delivered

**Documentation & Onboarding**

We completely reorganised [scalus.org](https://scalus.org/docs) with tailored learning paths for both Cardano developers transitioning to Scala and Scala developers entering Cardano. The [Language Guide](https://scalus.org/docs/language-guide) now includes detailed examples for every concept, and the [Smart Contracts Guide](https://scalus.org/docs/smart-contract) walks through the full development workflow—from writing to testing to deployment.

**Design Patterns & Security**

We implemented 6 of 9 design patterns from Anastasia Labs' catalogue, including Stake Validator, UTxO Indexer, and Transaction Level Minter patterns. Each comes with documentation, examples, and tests. We also added a [Plutus Vulnerabilities](https://scalus.org/docs/learning/plutus-vulnerabilities) guide to help developers avoid common security pitfalls.

**Rosetta Blueprints**

We delivered 7 of 21 Rosetta smart contract blueprints—practical implementations of common use cases like HTLC, Vesting, Vault, and Payment Splitter. These serve as production-ready references for developers building similar functionality.

**Developer Templates**

New command-line templates let developers scaffold projects instantly:
- `sbt new scalus3/hello.g8` – starter template
- `sbt new scalus3/validator.g8` – empty validator template

**Platform Improvements**

Based on community feedback, we refined the smart contract syntax, enhanced the standard library with new math utilities and optimised operations, and improved the test kit for better developer experience.

## Growing Adoption

We launched [Scalus Club](https://lu.ma/scalus)—a community of 30+ experienced Cardano developers meeting every 2-4 weeks for early access to features and direct input on the roadmap. Recent sessions covered [Smart Contract Development](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jSh97myCJwM) and [Transaction Building](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=soE7ExXu_vI).

Scalus is now consistently listed among the most actively developed projects on Cardano by [Proof of Cardano](https://x.com/ProofOfCardano).

## Why This Matters

Cardano needs robust developer tooling to grow. Scalus enables full-stack dApp development in a single language—smart contracts, transaction building, backend, and frontend—all in Scala 3. The resources delivered in this milestone lower the barrier to entry and give developers production-ready patterns to build on.

The milestone was reviewed and approved by No Witness Labs as third-party assurer, confirming deliverables align with scope and demonstrating that Intersect's milestone-based funding model works.

## What's Next

Milestone 3 will continue expanding the documentation, complete the remaining design patterns and Rosetta blueprints, and focus on deeper integrations and advanced tutorials.
